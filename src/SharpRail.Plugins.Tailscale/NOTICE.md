# Tailscale plugin

Original SharpRail implementation. This plugin invokes the user's installed Tailscale CLI; it does not
bundle Tailscale source, binaries or an SDK. The CLI remains separately installed and licensed by its distributor.

`assets/tailscale.svg` is the Tailscale icon from the official media kit linked at
https://tailscale.com/press, retrieved 2026-10-11:
https://cdn.sanity.io/files/w77i7m8x/production/a426ba5f63316745e108a18a6dbbfed6970752fd.zip
Archive entry: `Tailscale Logo/svg/Blk/Tailscale_icon_blk_rgb.svg`.
Its fill is changed from `#1e1e1f` to `currentColor` for theme contrast; geometry and opacity are unchanged.
Tailscale is a registered trademark of Tailscale Inc. The icon identifies the integration and does not imply endorsement.
